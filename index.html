TAKE ME TO

FIELD GUIDE Every tool. Every little possibility. +

Walk & explore

WASD to move, Shift to run, Space to jump. T lights your torch. F teleports to a random safe spot. The compass and map track points of interest. Cabins have sliding doors, furnished rooms and accessible upper floors.

Cast & catch

Go within 6 m of the pond or a riverbank. Press O to equip the rod, face the water and click to cast. When the bite prompt appears, press I within 2 seconds. O stows the rod.

Water & grow

Find the gardener near the flower beds during the day. Get close and press P to borrow the pot. Hold the left mouse button to pour onto the bed and watch the flowers bloom. Press P again to return it. Stow your rod first.

Fire & friendship

N sits in an empty chair; N again stands up. At night, come within 3 m of the fire and press Shift + O to grill up to 6 fish. They cook for 6 seconds, then your neighbors share the meal.

A living woodland

The gardener tends plants; the worker types at their desk. At dusk they come to the fire, sit and chat. Monkeys perch, swing and visit the ground. Seasons change foliage and snow; the sky carries the sun, moon, stars and constellations.

Your own rhythm

Pause time without stopping exploration. Change day length, seasons, weather and time here. Winter brings snow-covered terrain and snowfall; autumn has falling leaves and a leaf-covered forest floor. Spring blooms with cherry blossoms and flowers. Choose automatic weather or clear skies, clouds, fog, showers and thunderstorms. All audio is local: positional river and fire sounds, surface-aware footsteps, birds, wind, splashes, door movement and thunder. Click the map to enlarge it.
